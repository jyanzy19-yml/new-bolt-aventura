# Skill: Level Design and Campaign Content

Preserve the approved seven-world, seven-fragment and planned 42-phase campaign. The documented macro-plan is six phases per world, five progression/exploration phases and a sixth culminating guardian/fragment/transitional phase; consult the approved blueprint before fixing details. Do not invent world names, sequence, lore or guardian proper names.

Each phase needs a stable ID, objective, narrative entry/exit, route/layout, mechanic focus, encounter roster, hazards, platforms, checkpoint, secrets/rewards, difficulty curve, ability dependencies, mobile readability, performance budget and acceptance tests.

Use teach → practice → combine → mastery. Make hazards readable and counterplay learnable. Design for touch precision; avoid blind punishment, unavoidable damage, impossible recovery and softlocks. Recolors do not count as distinct archetypes. The documented target is at least five meaningfully distinct enemy archetypes per world.

Use blockout to validate geometry and flow; never label it final content. Test route, checkpoints, death/respawn, abilities, encounters, secrets, completion, camera bounds, mobile readability and performance. Read MASTER_DIRECTIVE.md and current campaign sources first.
