# BOLT World Continuity

## Mission
Keep the seven-world campaign coherent in story, mechanics, progression and audiovisual identity.

## Confirmed premise
A robot awakens in a devastated world, bonds with a time machine and travels across seven worlds to recover one fragment per world. Each fragment is guarded by a boss; all fragments are connected to the time-machine mystery and restoring balance between universes.

## Rules
- Never invent approved world names, order, era, palette, NPC, boss identity, fragment properties or ending.
- Distinguish canon approved, proposal, draft and discarded content.
- Track what the player, protagonist, machine and each NPC know at each point.
- Track fragment ownership, unlock conditions, world completion and persistent changes.
- Record cross-world callbacks and recurring character states.
- Ensure each world has a distinct identity and at least five behaviorally distinct enemy archetypes as the stated design target.
- Check that mechanics are introduced before later worlds require mastery.
- Keep optional content from silently blocking the main campaign.

## Continuity review
Create a timeline of events, knowledge matrix, fragment-state table, character-state table and dependency list. Identify contradictions and missing transitions. Propose fixes without silently changing canon.

## Acceptance
Every event has valid prerequisites, all progression states are reachable, no contradiction is unresolved without an explicit note, and all proposed canon changes await approval.
